Wait, do I actually get extra off this?

A short teardown of Student Beans' discount feed — where it breaks down for users, and a simple fix.

The problem

Student Beans exists to give students a verified discount on top of what everyone else already gets. But the app's discount feed shows student-exclusive offers and standard, publicly available discounts in the exact same card style, in the same scrollable list — with no consistent way to tell them apart at a glance.

In practice, that means a verified student can open the app, tap into an offer, and still not be sure whether their verification is actually doing anything for them.

Why it matters

This isn't just a cosmetic gap. Verification is the one thing Student Beans asks of a user and the one thing that differentiates it from a generic voucher app. If a student can't tell whether verifying actually earned them anything extra, the app's core value proposition quietly stops registering — even while the discounts themselves are perfectly real.

The fix

Two small changes: a consistent badge that marks which offers are student-exclusive, and a filter so a user can see only the offers their verification actually unlocks.

How I'd know it worked

Three signals I'd track after shipping this, in order of how directly they measure the actual confusion:

Redemption rate

Uplift in redemptions on badged "exclusive" offers, since users can now find them without hunting.

Support queries

Drop in tickets mentioning confusion over discount eligibility or verification value.

Clarity score

A short in-app survey asking users if they can tell which offers need verification — before and after.

This is a small interaction problem, not a redesign — which is exactly why it's easy to miss and cheap to fix.

It's also the kind of gap I look for by default: the product is doing its job, but the interface isn't telling the user it's working.
